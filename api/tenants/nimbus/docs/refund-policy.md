# Refund policy

Nimbus offers refunds on paid invoices within 14 days of the invoice date. Refunds go back to the original payment method and take 5 to 10 business days to appear.

## What can be refunded
- Monthly subscription charges, in full, within 14 days.
- Annual subscriptions: a prorated refund for unused full months, within 14 days of renewal.
- Accidental seat additions: the cost of the extra seats for the current period.

## What cannot be refunded
- Invoices older than 14 days, unless there was a billing error on our side. A human agent reviews those cases.
- Usage-based add-ons (automation runs, extra storage) that were already consumed.
- Invoices that are unpaid, void, or already refunded.

Support agents can approve refunds up to $500. Anything larger goes to the billing team.
