# Warranty on gear

Cartwheel-brand gear has a 2-year warranty against defects in materials and workmanship, such as failed seams, broken zippers or a tent that leaks at the seams. Normal wear and tear and damage from misuse aren't covered.

Within 30 days of delivery, a defective item can simply be returned for a refund. After that, warranty claims are handled by our team: escalate to a person and include photos of the defect.
