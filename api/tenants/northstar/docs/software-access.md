# Requesting software and app access

Standard apps (Google Workspace, Slack, Zoom, Okta) are set up when you join. Other apps need a request:

- **Figma, Miro, Notion:** approved by IT for any team that needs them.
- **Salesforce, NetSuite:** need your manager's approval as well; IT checks with them.
- **GitHub Enterprise:** engineering and design only.

Ask the helpdesk for access and say what you need it for. Most requests are approved within one business day and the app appears in your Okta dashboard.
