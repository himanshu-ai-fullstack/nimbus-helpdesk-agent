# Adding and removing seats

Each member with edit access uses one seat. Guests with view-only access are free on Pro and Business.

To add seats, invite a member from **Settings > Members**. The new seat is billed immediately with proration. To remove seats, remove or deactivate members, then lower the seat count under **Settings > Billing > Seats**. Removed seats are credited on your next invoice, not refunded.

If you added seats by mistake, contact support within 14 days and we can refund the extra seats for the current period.
