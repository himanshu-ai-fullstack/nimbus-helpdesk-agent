# Shipping options and delivery times

Cartwheel ships to all 50 US states, including Alaska and Hawaii. We do not ship internationally yet, so we can't deliver to Canada, Mexico or other countries.

- **Standard shipping:** free on orders over $75, otherwise $6.95. Arrives in 4 to 7 business days.
- **Expedited shipping:** $14.95. Arrives in 2 to 3 business days.
- **Overnight:** $29.95 for orders placed before 1 pm Mountain Time on a business day.

Orders leave our Denver warehouse within one business day. Large items such as kayaks ship by freight and take 7 to 10 business days.
