# Plans and pricing

Nimbus has three plans.

- **Free**: up to 3 members, 5 projects, 100 MB of storage, and 7 days of activity history.
- **Pro**: $12 per seat per month, or $120 per seat per year. Unlimited projects, 50 GB of storage, timeline view, guest access, and 250 automation runs per month.
- **Business**: $24 per seat per month, or $240 per seat per year. Everything in Pro, plus SAML SSO, audit logs, advanced permissions, 2,000 automation runs per month, and priority support.

Annual billing saves about 17% compared with monthly billing. Prices are in USD and exclude local taxes.
