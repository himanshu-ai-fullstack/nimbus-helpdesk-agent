# Leave and vacation policy

Full-time employees get **25 days of paid vacation** per calendar year, plus public holidays for their office location.

- Up to 5 unused vacation days roll over to the next year and must be used by March 31.
- Sick leave is separate: 10 paid days per year, with a doctor's note needed after 3 consecutive days.
- Parental leave is 20 weeks paid for every new parent.

Request leave in Workday under **Time Off**. Your manager approves it.
