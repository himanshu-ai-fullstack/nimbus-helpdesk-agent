# Service status and outages

Live service status is published at status.nimbus.example. If Nimbus is slow or unavailable, check the status page first.

During an incident, support will escalate your report to the on-call engineering team and add you to updates. Business plan customers affected by an outage longer than 4 hours in a month are eligible for a service credit of 10% of that month's fee. Credits are applied to the next invoice and are not paid out as refunds.
