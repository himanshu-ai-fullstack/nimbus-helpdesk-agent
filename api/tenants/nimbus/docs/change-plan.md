# Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your plan

Workspace owners and billing admins can change the plan from **Settings > Billing > Plan**.

## Upgrading
Upgrades take effect immediately. You are charged a prorated amount for the rest of the current billing period.

## Downgrading
Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period. If your workspace uses features that the lower plan does not include, such as SSO, those features are turned off when the downgrade takes effect. Data is never deleted on downgrade, but projects above the Free plan limit become read-only.

## Cancelling
Choose **Cancel subscription** at the bottom of the Plan page. Your workspace stays on the paid plan until the period ends, then moves to Free. Cancelling does not refund the current period automatically; see the refund policy.
