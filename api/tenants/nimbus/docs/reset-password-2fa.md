# Reset your password or two-factor authentication

To reset your password, click **Forgot password** on the sign-in page. The reset link expires after 30 minutes.

If you lost your two-factor device, sign in with one of the recovery codes you saved when you turned on 2FA. If you have no recovery codes, a workspace owner can reset 2FA for you from **Settings > Members**. Owners who lose 2FA must contact support and verify their identity; for security, support agents cannot reset 2FA in chat, so this is always handed to the security team.
