# Tracking your order

Order statuses mean:

- **Processing:** we have your order and are picking it. You can still cancel it.
- **Shipped:** it has left the warehouse. Your tracking number is in the shipping email and in your account under **Orders**.
- **Delivered:** the carrier marked it as delivered.

If tracking shows delivered but you can't find the package, check with neighbours and your building's mail room, then wait 48 hours: carriers sometimes mark packages delivered early. After 48 hours, contact us and we will open a claim with the carrier.
