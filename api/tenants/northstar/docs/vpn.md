# Connecting to the VPN

You need the VPN to reach internal tools (Jira, the build farm, the HR portal) from outside a Northstar office.

1. Install **GlobalProtect** from Self Service (macOS) or Software Center (Windows).
2. Enter the portal address `vpn.northstar.example`.
3. Sign in with your Northstar email and approve the Okta Verify push.

If it says "gateway not responding", switch from Wi-Fi to a phone hotspot to rule out a network that blocks VPNs, then restart GlobalProtect. If you still can't connect after that, IT can open a ticket for you.
