# Export your data

Workspace owners can export all projects, tasks, comments, and attachments from **Settings > Workspace > Export**. Exports are generated as a ZIP of CSV and JSON files and emailed to you within an hour. Large workspaces can take up to 24 hours.

Exports include data from archived projects. Activity history is included only for the retention period of your plan (7 days on Free, unlimited on Pro and Business).
